The local adapter keeps seven states and a stable 4-second main beat;
working gently breathes with a stable face and upper-right +, −, ×, ÷ operators
advancing once per second over a complete 4-second cycle; raised claws retain their 2x2
base shape; error has a fixed bold 5x5 pixel red X badge.
